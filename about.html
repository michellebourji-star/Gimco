Our story

A family business since 1997

GIMCO Petroleum opened its doors in Sfayla in 1997, and it has been run by the same family ever since. Over the years the station has grown from a place to fill up into a full automotive shop, with floor mats, filters, car care, truck lights, tools and much more under one roof.

What hasn't changed is how we treat people. Every member of our family works with customers personally: we remember your car, we take the time to find the right part, and we give you honest advice like we would to a neighbor. For us, every customer is a person, not a number.

Whether you stop for fuel, a quick oil filter or a full set of tools, you'll be served by people who care. Thank you for trusting us for more than 25 years.
